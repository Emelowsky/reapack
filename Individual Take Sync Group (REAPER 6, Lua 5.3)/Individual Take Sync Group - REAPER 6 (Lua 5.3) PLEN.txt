Individual Take Sync Group - wersja dla REAPERa 6 (Lua 5.3)
=============================================================

[PL]
Plik .lua w tym folderze to bytecode Lua 5.3 - działa TYLKO w REAPERze 6.x.
REAPER 7 ma Lua 5.4 - tam używamy folderu "Lua 5.4 (REAPER 7)".

Instalacja:
1. REAPER > Options > Show REAPER resource path... > Scripts > utwórz folder
   "Individual Take Sync Group" i skopiuj do niego plik .lua z tego folderu.
2. Wymagane: ReaImGui (Extensions > ReaPack > Browse packages > ReaImGui) - najnowszy,
   jaki ReaPack pokaże dla tej wersji REAPERa. Opcjonalnie js_ReaScriptAPI (okna "zawsze na
   wierzchu" bez migania, tylko nad REAPERem).
3. Actions > Show action list > New action > Load ReaScript... >
   "Emelowsky - Individual Take Sync Group.lua".
4. (Opcjonalnie) Ikona na pasek narzędzi: rozpakuj
   "Emelowsky - Individual Take Sync Group (toolbar icon).zip" do folderu zasobów REAPERa,
   potem Customize toolbar > Add > akcja skryptu > Icon... > "Emelowsky - Individual Take Sync Group".

ReaPack (zamiast kroków 1 i 3): Import repositories > https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Emelowsky/reapack/main/index.xml,
potem Browse packages > "Emelowsky - Individual Take Sync Group (REAPER 6, Lua 5.3)" > Install. Plik trafia do
Scripts/Emelowsky/Individual Take Sync Group (REAPER 6, Lua 5.3)/, a akcję ReaPack dodaje sam.

[EN]
The .lua file in this folder is Lua 5.3 bytecode - it works ONLY in REAPER 6.x.
REAPER 7 uses Lua 5.4 - use the "Lua 5.4 (REAPER 7)" folder there.

Installation:
1. REAPER > Options > Show REAPER resource path... > Scripts > create a folder
   "Individual Take Sync Group" and copy the .lua file from this folder into it.
2. Required: ReaImGui (Extensions > ReaPack > Browse packages > ReaImGui) - the newest
   version ReaPack offers for this REAPER version. Optional: js_ReaScriptAPI (flicker-free
   "always on top" windows, only over REAPER).
3. Actions > Show action list > New action > Load ReaScript... >
   "Emelowsky - Individual Take Sync Group.lua".
4. (Optional) Toolbar icon: unzip "Emelowsky - Individual Take Sync Group (toolbar icon).zip"
   into the REAPER resource path, then Customize toolbar > Add > the script's action > Icon... >
   "Emelowsky - Individual Take Sync Group".

ReaPack (instead of steps 1 and 3): Import repositories > https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Emelowsky/reapack/main/index.xml,
then Browse packages > "Emelowsky - Individual Take Sync Group (REAPER 6, Lua 5.3)" > Install. The file goes to
Scripts/Emelowsky/Individual Take Sync Group (REAPER 6, Lua 5.3)/, and ReaPack adds the action itself.
