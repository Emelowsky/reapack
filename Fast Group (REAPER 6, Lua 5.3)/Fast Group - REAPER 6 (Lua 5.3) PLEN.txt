Fast Group - wersja dla REAPERa 6 (Lua 5.3)
============================================

[PL]
Pliki .lua w tym folderze to bytecode Lua 5.3 - działają TYLKO w REAPERze 6.x.
REAPER 7 ma Lua 5.4 - tam używamy folderu "Lua 5.4 (REAPER 7)".

Instalacja:
1. REAPER > Options > Show REAPER resource path... > Scripts > utwórz folder
   "Fast Group" i skopiuj do niego WSZYSTKIE TRZY pliki .lua z tego folderu
   (Core, (manager), (Popup) - muszą leżeć razem).
2. Wymagane: ReaImGui (Extensions > ReaPack > Browse packages > ReaImGui) - najnowszy,
   jaki ReaPack pokaże dla tej wersji REAPERa. Opcjonalnie js_ReaScriptAPI (okna "zawsze na
   wierzchu" bez migania) i SWS.
3. Actions > Show action list > New action > Load ReaScript... >
   "Emelowsky - Fast Group (manager).lua" i osobno "Emelowsky - Fast Group (Popup).lua".
   Pliku "...Core.lua" NIE wczytuj jako akcji - to biblioteka, uruchomiona nic nie otwiera.
4. (Opcjonalnie) Ikona na pasek narzędzi: rozpakuj "Emelowsky - Fast Group (toolbar icon).zip"
   do folderu zasobów REAPERa, potem Customize toolbar > Add > akcja Managera > Icon... >
   "Emelowsky - Fast Group".

ReaPack (zamiast kroków 1 i 3): Import repositories > https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Emelowsky/reapack/main/index.xml,
potem Browse packages > "Emelowsky - Fast Group (REAPER 6, Lua 5.3)" > Install. Pliki trafiają do
Scripts/Emelowsky/Fast Group (REAPER 6, Lua 5.3)/, a obie akcje (Manager i Popup) ReaPack dodaje sam.

[EN]
The .lua files in this folder are Lua 5.3 bytecode - they work ONLY in REAPER 6.x.
REAPER 7 uses Lua 5.4 - use the "Lua 5.4 (REAPER 7)" folder there.

Installation:
1. REAPER > Options > Show REAPER resource path... > Scripts > create a folder
   "Fast Group" and copy ALL THREE .lua files from this folder into it
   (Core, (manager), (Popup) - they must sit together).
2. Required: ReaImGui (Extensions > ReaPack > Browse packages > ReaImGui) - the newest
   version ReaPack offers for this REAPER version. Optional: js_ReaScriptAPI (flicker-free
   "always on top" windows) and SWS.
3. Actions > Show action list > New action > Load ReaScript... >
   "Emelowsky - Fast Group (manager).lua" and separately "Emelowsky - Fast Group (Popup).lua".
   Do NOT load "...Core.lua" as an action - it is a library and opens nothing when run.
4. (Optional) Toolbar icon: unzip "Emelowsky - Fast Group (toolbar icon).zip" into
   the REAPER resource path, then Customize toolbar > Add > the Manager action > Icon... >
   "Emelowsky - Fast Group".

ReaPack (instead of steps 1 and 3): Import repositories > https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Emelowsky/reapack/main/index.xml,
then Browse packages > "Emelowsky - Fast Group (REAPER 6, Lua 5.3)" > Install. The files go to
Scripts/Emelowsky/Fast Group (REAPER 6, Lua 5.3)/, and ReaPack adds both actions (Manager and Popup) itself.
